## About

This site serves as a living collection of information on bypassing digital restrictions, maintaining privacy online, and advocating for evidence-based internet policy. The goal is not to encourage rule-breaking, but to inform and empower those who believe that access to information is a fundamental right.

All content, unless otherwise noted, is authored by **ignoblia**.
